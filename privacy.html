Privacy policy

Options Yield

Last updated October 16, 2026.

Options Yield is published by Atanas Lazarov. This policy describes what the app does with information. It is not legal advice.

What stays on your iPhone

Contract price, number of contracts, strike, cash tied up, and expiration are used only to calculate a result on your device. Your broker choice and appearance setting are saved only on your iPhone. The app does not create an account, and it does not send those numbers to us. We do not sell information and we do not show ads.

Data sources: stock prices and earnings

When you look up a stock, the app asks Yahoo Finance for the current price and asks Nasdaq for an earnings date. Those companies receive the ticker, or the date being checked, and your IP address as part of a normal internet request. We do not receive or store a copy of those requests. Their own policies apply to what they keep: Yahoo privacy policy and Nasdaq privacy statement.

Prices may be delayed and are for information only. Options Yield is not investment advice and does not place trades.

Tracking

The app does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites, and it does not use advertising identifiers.

Children

Options Yield is not directed at children under 13.
