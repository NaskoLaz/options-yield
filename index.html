Options Yield

Options Yield is a calculator for covered calls and cash-secured puts. It shows the premium after a $0.65 per-contract fee (based on Fidelity), the cash the trade ties up, and the return for that expiration, plus weekly, monthly, and annualized figures.

The app does not place trades. It is not investment advice. The annualized figure assumes the same premium could be repeated through the year. Actual results will vary.

Help

  • Enter the contract price and the number of contracts.
  • Pick a stock, or type a ticker, to load the current price.
  • Choose an expiration on the calendar. Bold green dates are the third Friday.
  • Live prices and earnings dates need an internet connection.
